Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective & Updated: October 2026

1. Open-Access Zero-Barrier Policy

NotesPoint is built as an open educational platform. Students can browse classes, open subjects, and read PDF notes without mandatory personal data collection or paywalls.

2. Local & Cloud Data Storage

Study preferences (such as your display name, daily study timer progress, and bookmarked notes) are stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage. Public reviews and support requests are securely managed via Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.

3. Safe In-Browser PDF Rendering

All educational PDFs hosted on our Cloudinary CDN are streamed through Mozilla's PDF.js HTML5 canvas engine directly inside your browser, isolating your device from third-party executable downloads.

4. Direct Contact & Takedown Desk

For academic inquiries, missing chapter requests, or copyright questions, reach our official support desk at notespoint2026@gmail.com.
