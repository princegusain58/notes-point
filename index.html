Privacy Policy & Support Desk
1. Zero-Barrier Educational Access

NotesPoint Academy provides free study notes, NCERT solutions, and competitive examination resources for Class 1–12 and government job aspirants without mandatory registration or hidden charges.

2. Data Collection & Privacy Protection

We do not sell or share student information with third-party advertisers. Bookmarks, theme preferences, and recent search filters are stored locally on your device. Reviews and support messages submitted voluntarily are stored securely on Google Firebase Cloud Firestore for moderation and display.
